Also said about the work

Questions worth asking first

How fast can we go live?
Most coaches are live in about 30 days — design, copy, tech and automations included. The 90-day sprint keeps iterating after that.
Do you run ads?
Not first. We build organic and partner traffic before anyone spends on ads, because ads to a funnel that doesn't convert just makes the loss faster. Ads stay optional, later.
What happens on the first call?
Fifteen minutes on your niche, your offer, your pricing and the fastest honest path to 10–15 calls a month. If a smaller step is smarter than working with us, we'll say so.
Do I own what you build?
Yes — files, logins, copy, automations and SOPs, all of it, handed over at the end. Nothing is locked to a platform we control.
Do I need a website to start?
No. If you're early we'll ship a minimal funnel and book calls first. Website polish can follow once there's something to polish it for.

Going quiet is fixable. It's usually the order that's wrong, not the effort.

Fifteen minutes, no pitch. Bring what you've got — a half-finished site, a list you stopped emailing, an offer you're not sure about — and we'll tell you what to do next even if that's nothing to do with us.
