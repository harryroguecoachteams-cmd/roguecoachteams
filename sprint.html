The timeline

Three phases. Fast to launch, then compounding.

Weeks 1–4

Map & build

  • Offer and niche clarity, KPI targets, a short backlog
  • Brand mini-kit: voice, topics, content system
  • Home and offer page, opt-in through to the calendar

Weeks 5–8

Ship & optimize

  • Weekly content, distribution and repurposing
  • Email sequence, lead magnet, tech QA
  • Warm-intro JV shortlist and outreach scripts

Weeks 9–12

Scale & hand off

  • Tune the conversion paths, ship the final assets
  • Partnership calls and follow-ups
  • Handover SOPs and the 30-day support window

Typical outcome in 90 days is 10–15 qualified calls a month, depending on your niche and offer. Maria's words on pace are worth repeating: the growth is rarely overnight.
